# Tunis Game Hub

Create a modern, dark, premium website for "The Game Production", the ultimate indoor entertainment complex in Tunis, Tunisia.



Real business details:

- Location: Route de la Marsa GP9, Ain Zaghouane, Tunis 1100

- Phone / WhatsApp: +216 20 641 111

- Open every day

- 1000 m² space combining Escape Rooms, Game On Coffee Shop, and The Game Park activities



Style:

- Dark cinematic theme (deep black, neon purple, electric blue accents)

- Energetic, fun, premium and immersive atmosphere

- Fully mobile responsive

- Clean modern design with smooth animations



Structure (one long scrolling page):



1. Hero

- Headline: "The ultimate destination for fun in Tunis"

- Subtext: "Escape rooms • Board games • Axe throwing • Interactivity • Karaoke & more — all in one place"

- Location badge: Ain Zaghouane • Route de la Marsa

- Two buttons: "Book Now" and "Discover Activities"

- Strong stats: 1000 m² • Open daily • Perfect for friends, families & team building



2. What we offer (main activities)

Show clear cards or sections for:

- Escape Room Tunisia (immersive rooms: Prison Break, Sabotage, Virus, The Hangover Hotel…)

- Game On Coffee Shop (400+ board games + café)

- Axe Throwing

- Interactivity

- Chill Room / Nest Room

- Karaoke & other games



3. Escape Rooms section

Highlight the main rooms with short descriptions, duration, players and difficulty. Include "Book this room" buttons.



4. Game On Coffee Shop

- Board games café atmosphere

- Food & drinks

- Perfect place to continue the fun after activities



5. Gallery

Use realistic photos of the real venue (axe throwing, rooms, people having fun, café, neon lights, teams celebrating)



6. Testimonials

4–5 realistic short reviews with Tunisian names



7. Booking / Contact form

- Full name, Phone (+216), Activity choice, Number of people, Preferred date & time

- Big WhatsApp + "Request booking" buttons

- Note that they confirm by phone/WhatsApp



8. FAQ

Common questions: booking, age limit, group size, is it suitable for kids/corporate, etc.



9. Contact & Location

- Phone: +216 20 641 111

- WhatsApp button

- Address: Route de la Marsa GP9, Ain Zaghouane, Tunis

- Opening hours (open daily)

- Embedded Google Map

- Social icons (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok)



Footer: © 2026 The Game Production



Important instructions:

- Make the design feel energetic and premium, not generic AI

- Prioritize conversion (booking is the main goal)

- Use realistic, high-quality images of entertainment venues (axe throwing, neon, groups of friends, café)

- Keep text natural and persuasive in English (or mix with a bit of French if needed)
